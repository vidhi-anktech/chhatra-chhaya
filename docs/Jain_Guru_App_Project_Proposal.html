Poojya Acharya Shri 108 Vibhav Sagarji Maharaj App

Comprehensive Feature Transcription, System Architecture & Finalized Scope Document
Executive Overview: A dedicated, ad-free Digambar Jain spiritual mobile application engineered with React Native Expo, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Firebase on a 100% free-tier architecture (/bin/zsh/month).

1. Finalized Architectural Decisions (Post-Meeting)

  • Frictionless Devotee Onboarding: General users require NO password or OTP. On first launch, they enter Name, Age, and City and immediately access all features.
  • In-App Admin & Sangh Access: No separate web portal. Sangh coordinators and administrators securely log in directly within the mobile app to post Vihar notices, upload discourse links, and manage gallery photos.
  • Splash → Language Selection: First launch presents a language picker (Hindi, English, Gujarati; default Hindi), switchable anytime in Settings.
  • Continuous Ambient Background Audio: Soft ambient spiritual flute/chanting plays across app navigation, with auto-pause during media playback and an instant mute button in the header.
  • Home Screen 'Dainik Niyam' & Live Anumodna Wall: Devotees select their daily vow (e.g. Ratri Bhojan Tyag). Their name and city appear publicly in real time for spiritual rejoicing (अनुमोदना), followed by a morning check-in prompt the next day.
  • Shravak Devotion Leaderboard: An engagement board in Meri Sadhna recognizing devotees based on Navkar Mala counts, Swadhyay reading minutes, Pratikraman completions, and Niyam streaks.
  • Focused V1 Scope: 5 features have been deferred or merged (9 Bhaktis & Chhahdhala, Seva & Jeevdaya, Aaj Ka Sankalp/Ek Achha Karya, and Abhakshya Food Scanner) to maintain a fast, high-quality initial release.
